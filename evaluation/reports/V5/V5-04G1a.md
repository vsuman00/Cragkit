# V5-04G1a: Native managed repair

Status: COMPLETE for G1a. This slice adds public repair for prepared generations and uncertain stage writes whose complete physical replica can be verified. G1b must still implement recovery writes for genuinely incomplete replicas. Security convergence, purge and full G/H/V5 acceptance remain incomplete.

For example, a prepared G2 can survive recovery while G1 stays active. Repair retains G2's original owner, intent, generation, preparation and expected pointer, obtains a fresh ordinary readback receipt, records the current attestation and publishes only after final authorization and fence checks.

## Behavior and boundaries

Both native managers expose `repair(reference, scope, ledger, deadline/options)`. The reference has exactly the publish fields: `collection`, `document_id`, `intent_id`. Trusted tenant scope stays separate. Results retain the existing nine fields with `operation="repair"`. This profile requires the exact attestation schema and native supervisor/recovery pair. Worker repair is explicitly unsupported; publish is unchanged.

The writer preflight locks backend, owner and document in the established order, verifies the immutable graph and original fences, and preserves terminal history. It can inspect a blocked instance without claiming physical completion or permission to write. Changed fences produce denied/conflict outcomes; another intent's owner is never adopted.

Uncertainty requires actual recovery fencing, advancement of the same target owner, full readback and retired settlement. Reopening still checks the entire instance. Foreign uncertainty stays blocked without adopting another scope. After complete verified settlement and reopening, a writing target may repeat the bounded ordinary point sequence to establish a current dispatch binding. This is deliberate idempotent replay. Prepared and attestation retries do not resend points. Partial readback remains a failure until G1b supplies the recovery dispatcher.

Cross-epoch prepared repair obtains actual F1 verification and holds actual finalization across reauthorization, exact attestation insertion/confirmation and publication. Stable private verification IDs permit retry after interruption. A stale credential on an existing immutable attestation is unavailable; repair cannot overwrite it or allocate another owner to evade it. The original deadline, cancellation latch and one logical ledger attempt cover the whole operation.
