# V5-04D: Native publication authority

State: COMPLETE for authoritative writer transactions. Full V5 and V5-04 remain incomplete until their later acceptance gates pass. Baseline: `0bcb7fb`.

For example, two writers can prepare against the same active generation. When one publishes, the other records a conflict and preserves the winner. Retrying an earlier successful publication returns its historical receipt; it does not republish that generation.

## Implemented

Both native internal writer modules provide explicit initialization, immutable stage admission, intent lookup, verified stage reconstruction, document ownership, exact dispatch reservation and outcome recording, preparation/publication, immediate security changes and tombstone-bound purge admission. They borrow pools and perform no Qdrant HTTP, authorization callback or model call. The public manager remains the later F/G work.

Stage admission binds B2 source/member/intent identity and every B1 vector seal. Real profiles require full E-r1 batches; raw vector arrays are restricted to the fake profile. Original binary64 sidecars survive restart, including values whose rounded representation would fail original-input admission. A loaded stage is privately issued after full source, membership, manifest, claim and sidecar verification.

Transactions use READ COMMITTED, parameterized trusted scope settings and bounded leases. Initialization checks exact publication history, catalog and structural constraints. Every lease checks the dedicated writer role against fixed table, column and helper permissions, safe capability flags and absence of outbound capability memberships. Named capability grants are not trusted as their own expected baseline.

New dispatch admission checks current security fences. Recording an already performed request's known completion or uncertainty does not require the old security revision; it still requires the current owner and backend epoch. Unknown work cannot be redispatched or settled by the ordinary writer. A new reservation and preparation fail operationally while earlier work is unresolved.

Final publication checks original fences before locking, then locks backend, owner and document and checks fresh facts again. Known authority changes persist `denied`; a changed active pointer persists `conflict`. Owner replacement alone is operational failure. Immediate revocation/tombstoning does not acquire vector lifecycle locks.
