Date: 24 October 2026. State: COMPLETE for V2-06A-D and V2-C2. Scope: internal candidate adapters and pure fusion in native Python and TypeScript. Public hybrid orchestration remains V2-07; V2 as a whole is in progress.

## Delivered behavior

For example, `ERR_CONN-42` stays one exact identifier even when full-text search splits its punctuation. The versioned ASCII heuristic preserves case, deduplicates tokens and excludes ordinary words. Both lexical and vector indexers populate the existing identifier array. Exact candidate queries use full composite joins, active ready generations, trusted tenant/collection, current authorization revision, principal/grants, tombstone checks and required filters before LIMIT. Source fetching validates identifier metadata and reauthorizes exact immutable source in a fresh transaction. Identifier selection needs no pgvector capability.

[IF-r1](../../../contracts/IDENTIFIER_FUSION_V2.md) freezes the extraction, candidate and fusion contracts. Pure weighted reciprocal rank fusion deduplicates each list, ranks distinct candidates from 1, normalizes family weights per facet and retains each family's best rank across repeated lists. Raw scores remain diagnostic provenance. Canonical list/identity ordering, UTF-8 tie-breaking and ordered sums keep the runtimes aligned. Conflicting immutable metadata fails with `IntegrityViolation`; invalid inputs, union overflow and positive-vote underflow fail with `InvalidRequest`.

A small example explains the choice: with equal dense/lexical weights and smoothing 60, rank1 in both lists gets `1/61`, while rank1 in only one gets `0.5/61`. Adding a duplicate occurrence or replaying the same family does not create another vote. A failure case is two distinct candidates with `max_candidates=1`: fusion rejects the union rather than silently dropping evidence. Scores are ranking values, not confidence or source truth.

Historical immutable generations and receipt hashes remain unchanged. Old empty identifier arrays require a new ingestion intent/generation; no backfill is performed. The heuristic is ASCII only, may interpret punctuation ambiguously and ignores tokens over 256 bytes. It does not recognize every programming identifier. No new migration, setup artifact, schema, permanent grant, dependency or live model configuration was introduced.
