## Implemented behavior

Both optional PostgreSQL entry points export PostgresVectorIndexer and its receipt type. Stage persists immutable canonical/chunk/lexical records and bounded profile-bound vectors without moving the active pointer. Publish independently recomputes identity, verifies exact job/revision/generation/chunk manifest and matching counts, rechecks authorization after the hook, then changes readiness, pointer, metadata and job state atomically. Ingest retains one immutable input snapshot and one admission guard across both parts.

The first provisioned profile is the synthetic fake1536 identity in E-r1 and VG-r1. Same dimensions with another model do not establish compatibility. Unsupported or unavailable profile/catalog/history fails before embedding dispatch. Provider work runs outside the pooled lease and transaction. One indexer admits one operation without a waiting queue; at most 128 batches and 8 resume attempts, configurable downward. Item, byte/token, deadline and cancellation limits remain enforced.

A failed rebuild preserves the exact prior pointer and actual authorized lexical results. A stale publication cannot overwrite a newer winner. Retained immutable source excerpts preserve canonical UTF-8 spans and digests; source release rechecks current authorization. Revocation and tombstones remain denied even while old generations are retained.
